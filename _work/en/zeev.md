I worked as UX Designer in the Products & Development area of Zeev, a business process automation (BPM) platform, responsible for designing and evolving the user experience of strategic product modules. I worked on flows, information architecture, interfaces and reusable components, helping make the platform more intuitive, configurable and scalable for companies across different industries.

I was actively involved in creating new features for process management, dynamic forms, workflows and advanced settings, from problem discovery to final delivery with the product and development teams.

### Main activities

- Gathering requirements with product, business and clients.
- Mapping journeys and complex corporate process flows.
- Creating wireframes, clickable prototypes and high-fidelity interfaces.
- Structuring the information architecture of the platform's modules.
- Evolving the configuration experience for workflows, forms and permissions.
- Running usability validation and interface refinements.
- Defining visual patterns and reusable components.
- Daily collaboration with Product Managers, developers and stakeholders.
- Implementing and maintaining screens with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Bootstrap.
- Taking part in agile ceremonies using Scrum and Kanban.

### Project highlights

- Took part in creating Zeev's Design System in Figma.
- Visual standardization of the company's products, increasing consistency and scalability.
- Redesigned administrative and operational flows, reducing complexity for end users.
- Evolved the BPM process configuration experience, making advanced tasks more accessible.
- Integrated design and development to ensure faithful implementation of the interfaces.
- Supported the platform's shift to a more modern, modular and user-experience-oriented model.

### Business outcome

I contributed to the evolution of one of the leading process automation platforms in the Brazilian market, improving the usability of complex features, increasing the product's visual consistency and bringing design and development closer through shared patterns and reusable components.
