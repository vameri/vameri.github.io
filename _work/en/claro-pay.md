I worked as Product Designer on the evolution of the Claro Pay Super App, taking part in the modernization of the platform and in building a new digital experience for millions of Claro customers. I worked on initiatives for financial products and digital services, from discovery through delivery and validation of the solutions.

### Main responsibilities

- Running Discovery processes, identifying business opportunities and user needs.
- Planning and running qualitative and quantitative research.
- User interviews, behavioral analysis and journey mapping.
- Building personas and hypotheses and defining business problems.
- Information architecture for financial products and digital services.
- Creating flows, wireframes, prototypes and high-fidelity interfaces.
- Evolving and maintaining the Design System integrated into the Claro Brasil ecosystem.
- Facilitating Design Thinking workshops and co-creation sessions.
- Defining and tracking product, conversion and user experience metrics.
- Usability testing and continuous validation of solutions.
- Working closely with Product Managers, Tech Leads, developers and business areas in an agile environment.
