I worked as UX Designer at Datum TI on digital projects for web platforms and mobile apps, most notably the Sulgás digital transformation. I was responsible for designing the user experience, the information architecture and the interfaces of the portal and the Android and iOS apps, contributing to the modernization of the company's digital channels.

I worked across every stage of the design process, from understanding the needs of users and business areas to prototyping, validation and following up on implementation with the development teams.

### Main activities

- Gathering requirements with business areas and stakeholders.
- Mapping user journeys and flows for digital channels.
- Creating wireframes, prototypes and interfaces for web and mobile applications.
- Defining the information architecture of the digital products.
- Designing experiences for Android, iOS and web platforms.
- Creating components and visual patterns to ensure consistency across channels.
- Taking part in refinements and validations with the development teams.
- Following up on the implementation of the interfaces in production.
- Applying Design Thinking concepts and agile methodologies.

### Sulgás project highlights

- Took part in building the Sulgás Digital Portal.
- Designed the experiences of the Android and iOS apps for the company's customers.
- Evolved the inquiry, service and digital self-service flows.
- Standardized the experience across web and mobile platforms.
- Close collaboration between UX, business and development to deliver what users actually need.

### Results

- Modernized Sulgás's digital channels.
- Improved navigation and access to the company's services.
- Greater visual and functional consistency between the portal and the apps.
- Reduced usage barriers through more intuitive, user-oriented interfaces.
- Strengthened the company's digital strategy by adopting good UX and Product Design practices.

This experience consolidated my work on large-scale digital products, combining UX Design, UI Design and technical knowledge to deliver efficient solutions for both web platforms and mobile apps.
