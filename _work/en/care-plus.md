I worked as Product Designer and UX Designer on the continuous evolution of the Care Plus app for Android and iOS, leading research, information architecture, interface design and user experience optimization initiatives. I worked directly on identifying friction points in the digital journey, proposing user-centered solutions that led to simpler, more intuitive and more efficient flows.

### Main activities

- Mapping and analyzing the app's user journey.
- Creating and evolving navigation flows for Android and iOS.
- Designing wireframes, prototypes and high-fidelity interfaces.
- Reviewing and reorganizing the information architecture of the digital products.
- Running research and usability validation with users and stakeholders.
- Defining functional requirements together with business and technology teams.
- Creating and maintaining interface components and patterns to ensure visual consistency.
- Following up on development with the technology teams using agile methodology.
- Analyzing metrics and feedback to find opportunities for continuous improvement.
- Multidisciplinary collaboration with Product Owners, developers and business areas.

### Results

- Redesigned critical app flows, reducing the complexity of user journeys.
- Modernized the interfaces, making navigation more intuitive and accessible.
- Significantly improved the experience of sign-up, appointments, exams and digital services.
- Increased visual consistency between the Android and iOS platforms.
- Reduced doubts and usage barriers through a clearer, user-oriented experience.
- Strengthened the product strategy by applying UX and Design Thinking practices.

### Tools and technologies

Figma, Adobe XD, Sketch, Design System, Prototyping, UX Research, Usability Testing, Information Architecture, Design Thinking, Agile Methodologies (Scrum/Kanban), Xamarin (integration with the development team).
