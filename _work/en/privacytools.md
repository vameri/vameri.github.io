I worked as UX Designer and Product Designer at the PrivacyTools startup, leading the visual identity, user experience and MVP prototyping of the platform. I was involved from the earliest stage of product definition, helping turn the business vision into a digital solution for privacy management, data governance and LGPD (Brazil's data protection law) compliance.

I was responsible for structuring the user experience, defining the brand identity and creating the prototypes used to validate the product with investors, clients and the development team.

### Main activities

- Created the visual identity and initial brand positioning of PrivacyTools.
- Defined the user experience for the platform's MVP.
- Ran discovery and product alignment workshops.
- Gathered business requirements and translated them into digital solutions.
- Created the platform's information architecture.
- Designed wireframes and clickable prototypes.
- Defined navigation flows and user journeys.
- Designed interfaces focused on usability and product adoption.
- Supported product strategy and the validation of business hypotheses.
- Worked directly with founders, stakeholders and the technology team.

### Project highlights

- Complete creation of the PrivacyTools brand, including visual identity and digital experience.
- Designed and prototyped the MVP that served as the basis for building the platform.
- Took part in the strategic definition of a product for the LGPD and Data Governance market.
- Structured the solution's main modules before development started.
- Supported product presentations to investors and potential clients.

### Results

- Built an MVP capable of validating the startup's value proposition.
- Contributed to raising more than R$ 2 million in investment to evolve the product.
- Created a solid foundation for the platform's scalability.
- Structured the user experience in an emerging privacy and data protection market.
- Helped build a company that keeps growing and expanding in the PrivacyTech market.

### Business context

PrivacyTools was founded to help companies comply with the LGPD through a platform specialized in privacy governance and management. I joined in the startup's early stage, taking part in creating the brand, defining the product experience and building the MVP that helped validate the business model and drive its growth.
