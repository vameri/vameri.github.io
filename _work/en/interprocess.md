I worked as Product Designer on GEMED 2.1, a system developed by Interprocess to support the management of clinics and hospitals, with a special focus on processes related to oncology treatment.

The project involved a complex domain, with different user profiles, clinical and administrative flows and a large volume of information needed to follow up on patients. My role was to turn that complexity into clearer, more consistent and more efficient experiences for the professionals who use the system every day.

I worked end to end in the product process: understanding user and business needs, requirements analysis, flow definition, information architecture, wireframes, prototyping, interfaces and following up on development.

### AI integrated into the Product Design process

During the project, I brought Artificial Intelligence into my daily workflow, using it as a support tool across the different stages of the design process.

AI was used to analyze and organize requirements, explore scenarios and flows, structure information, raise hypotheses, review content and support the documentation of product decisions. It also helped explore interface alternatives and the communication between design and development.

This approach sped up operational tasks and freed more time for analyzing problems, user experience and product decisions, while keeping human validation at the center of the process.

My technical background in development also helped bring Design, AI and Engineering closer, making it possible to evaluate solutions in terms of both user experience and implementation feasibility.

The main challenge was dealing with the complexity of a healthcare system, where the experience has to balance a large volume of information, operational efficiency, error reduction, ease of use and the sensitivity of patient-care processes.

**Scope:** Product Design, UX/UI Design, AI applied to the design process, requirements analysis, information architecture, user flows, wireframes, prototyping, Design System, documentation, validation and development follow-up.
