I worked as UX Designer on the modernization of the trading desk back office at UBS Investment Bank, taking part in the migration of a legacy platform to a modern web solution for capital markets, equities and futures. The project involved transforming critical processes used every day by traders, operators, analysts and operational support teams.

My role was to connect the needs of business users with the technology teams, translating complex financial market operations into more intuitive, efficient and safe experiences.

### Main activities

- Running interviews and discovery sessions with operators, analysts and financial market specialists.
- Gathering and documenting requirements with the business areas.
- Mapping the trading desk's operational journeys and flows.
- Designing navigation flows for allocation, execution and trade monitoring processes.
- Creating low and mid-fidelity wireframes for quick validation of solutions.
- Building clickable prototypes to align business and technology.
- Structuring the information architecture of the new platform.
- Defining visual patterns and reusable components for the project's Design System.
- Facilitating workshops and validation sessions with end users.
- Following up on development and acceptance testing of deliveries with the technical teams.

### Technologies and environment

- React.js
- Java
- Oracle Database
- Figma
- UX Research
- Information Architecture
- Wireframes and Prototyping
- Design System
- Scrum and Kanban

### Main deliveries and results

- Took part in migrating a legacy system to a modern web platform.
- Simplified complex operational processes of the trading desk.
- Reduced the learning curve for new users of the system.
- Standardized the experience through reusable components.
- Improved operational efficiency in asset allocation and distribution processes.
- Greater alignment between business, UX and development throughout the product cycle.
- Built a scalable foundation for the future evolution of the bank's systems.

### Business context

The system supported operations in equities, variable income, futures and the distribution of domestic and international assets, backing critical Back Office and Middle Office activities in a highly complex and regulated environment. The project focused on increasing productivity, reducing operational errors and modernizing the user experience without compromising the robustness of financial processes.
