Technology is built in community: free knowledge, open source and collaboration.

Hello World! I'm Vagner Ribas, a Product Designer, Front End Developer and Product Engineer based in Porto Alegre, Brazil. I believe knowledge exists to be shared, and a big part of my journey has been built alongside the tech communities.

In 2010 I helped create Matehackers, the second hackerspace in Brazil, still active today at Vila Flores. I was a volunteer Mozillian, helping the community of people using Firefox and Mozilla's tools. Since then I've helped create, activate and run user groups in Rio Grande do Sul (from mobile and front end to UX and software architecture) and today I coordinate GUMA RS.

All of this work is volunteer work with a simple goal: sharing free knowledge. I'm dedicated to open source projects and free software, I contribute to open projects, help organize meetups and have mentored many people looking to start a career in technology. I'm enthusiastic about all kinds of technology, a maker who loves to collaborate and build all sorts of things.
