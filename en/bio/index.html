From packer to Product Designer: the long way into technology.

Hello World! My name is Vagner Ribas. Today I'm a Product Designer, Fullstack Developer, Scrum Master, agilist, Product Engineer and community manager based in Porto Alegre, Brazil, but before that happened I'd already done a few things.

At age 15, from 12/1997 to 12/1998, I had my first job as a packer at Zaffari. I worked as an office boy from 09/1999 to 01/2000, in an office that did the accounting for several day care centers. I also worked as a stock replenisher in a market (04/2001 – 11/2001) and as a bartender at a bingo (12/2001 – 02/2002).

My last job as an office boy was at CORECON/RS, the authority responsible for registering economists and regularizing professionals. I stayed there for almost 10 years and, in 2011, resigned as head of the registry sector.

In 2011 I started an internship as a Graphic Designer and Web Designer at DBServer, in the marketing area. That was my first contact with the technology area — and since then I've moved through web design, front end, UX, product design, business analysis and agile roles.
