I'm Vagner Ribas, a Product Designer, Front End Developer and Product Engineer specialized in AI, based in Porto Alegre, Brazil.

Product Designer passionate about building digital products that solve real problems and create impact for users and businesses. I work across the whole product development cycle: Discovery, user research and requirements definition, wireframes, prototyping, usability testing, Design Systems and following up on implementation with development teams.

I currently work as Scrum Master at Interprocess Gemed — Tecnologia em Saúde. My work sits between product design, fullstack development and agile practice, acting as Product Designer, Product Engineer, Scrum Master, agilist and community manager.

I've been working with interface design since 2008, in several companies, with a focus on interface design, planning, information architecture and development. I'm enthusiastic about different technologies and enjoy taking part in, and creating, community groups to share knowledge and build a network.

In 2011 I started an internship as a Graphic Designer and Web Designer at DBServer, in the marketing area — my first contact with the technology area. See my full portfolio (in Portuguese) or the complete bio.
